Keywords:

ℹ How keywords work
  • Matched as substrings of the merchant name, case-insensitively.
  • During import, the first matching category wins — match order is not alphabetical, it depends on when the category was created. Avoid overlapping keywords.
  • Example: if "cash" and "cashback" are in different categories, any merchant containing "cashback" will also trigger "cash". The category whose keyword was stored first wins.
  • More specific keywords (longer) are safer. Short keywords like "bar" or "gas" can produce false positives.
  • Duplicate keywords (exact match in two categories) are blocked. Substring overlaps show a warning.
